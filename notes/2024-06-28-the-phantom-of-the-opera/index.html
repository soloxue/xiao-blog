"The Phantom of the Opera" live at West End in His Majesty's Theatre!! Where the dream began 37 years ago, then Her Majesty's Theatre~

I'M STILL IN A DAZED AWE THAT THIS ACTUALLY HAPPENED

This was such a close call: Jon Robyns injured his foot last Wednesday and nearly couldn’t make it, and Joe had been travelling a couple of days ago and almost missed his principal performance. But all three principals are on today. All I can say is that I’m ridiculously lucky 😏❤️

I'm just back from stagedooring! Here are some of my notes/commentary (note that it's only some songs/bits I'm commenting on). I wrote this for my own reviewing purpose but I'll still REALLY appreciate if you guys give it a read~😘(I use character names and actor names interchangeably) And it's around 11 in the night rn so I'm being crazy with the writing

Auction and opening

James Gant was totally channeling the inner Phantom as Auctioneer when he got to the "Lot 666" part😂Gantom vibes👑(It's interesting how some Phantoms get to be Auctioneer, Earl Carpenter being all too iconic) I had previously worried if I'd be immune to Overture now that I'm hearing it so often but NOO the live version continues to astound me and I'm 100% grateful. You can only appreciate Overture if you actually listen to it live <3 Where I sit the chandelier rose right over my head and then swung upwards. I am the happiest person to exist on this Earth.✨

Piangi was super funny during Hanninal rehearsal when he flipped his cape and it landed on the guard's head😂I loved it when Carlotta teases André with her scarf. Think of Me sounded very comfortable, love Lily's voice and it's super perfect in the "there will never be a day" part, it's just very comfortable to hear, trust me. Her voice is so smooth and sweet! When she started her "audition" her voice was VERY quiet (barely audible), but once she got going she had such a beautiful crystal-clear tone. Effortless cadenza at the end.

I love the little interactions between Meg and Christine during "Angel of Music". It really shows that they are friends and more carefree with each other. They hug and Meg swings their held hands in "I only wish I knew your secret.❤️ Love this.

André (Adam Linstead) and Firmin (Matt Harrop) were SO FUNNY OMG André was such a fan over Carlotta, and Firmin was just a grumpy old man. And they were super awesome in Notes I, they simultaneously leveled themselves up to the table and sat on it in unison, they're literally mirrors! And BRUNETTE MEG WAS SO GOOD (Leroux-accurate!!) Maiya has a little frustrated remark after Madame Giry tells her to go off and practice, it was something like "There's always more practice!" I thought it was cool that they gave her a bit more character. And Adam is HILARIOUS he was shaking like a leaf during Il Muto when he had to go out and announce the change in plan, flipping through his programme nervously. And his antics with the ballet dancers seemed so naturally clumsy! Also the vast difference in the physique and age of the managers made them fairly easy to tell apart, FOR ONCE, especially during the non-Masquerade scenes

The Mirror, title song and vibrato

Lily locks the door between "Things have changed, Raoul" and "Insolent Boy". She does NOT want Raoul coming back. She is seemingly delighted to hear the "voice" and is very willing going through the mirror. Low growl-ish line in "I am your Angel of Music". Pure gold🤌Title song ("The Phantom of the Opera") classic as always. I have listened to and watched this part for countless times but I was still in perfect awe when the boat appeared and the candelabra rose out of the ground, as if I was seeing it for the first time. That's why Phantom is the #1 best in the world, not accepting other opinions😌During journey to the Lair, Jon does a full cape flip. You can clearly hear Jon was influenced by Killian Donnelly (who brought him into Phantom so not surprising). Both of them like to do terminal vibrato, like the vibrato is at the end of "find" in "for now I find", and "hear" and "it's me they hear". Jon's "inside" of "inside your mind" is so perfect the latter part is a half whispered growl/hiss and it's so gentle and so vicious at the same time🤣I love Lily's "I am the mask you wear", her voice is so beautiful and the transition of "I" to "am" is perfect.

And the two of them are so simultaneous, all their uses of vibrato and crescendos and tones are so perfectly coordinated!! Lily has such effortless high notes, its amount of vibrato is quite good, although I'd love to hear a bit more. Jon is right in the middle on the Michael↔Ramin scale of aggressiveness, which makes him quite Leroux-accurate, which is explained in more detail down there somewhere. He can be real scary but also real gentle in a single line, which is pretty hard to do but I think he nailed it. Still in perfect awe.

Note from☝: This terminal vibrato may be quite new? The most comparable is "the *Phantom* of the Opera is there" where the vibrato appears around the fourth beat of "Phaaaan~~tom" in recent recordings and videos. It varies sometimes because of different stanzas and singers and sometimes it's not there at all (e.g. Christine's first stanza, Lily completely skips vibrato here and sometimes it's just not as intense, à la Lucy St. Louis) and sometimes it's a full vibrato or appears early around the third beat. Killian usually skips vibrato in his first stanza ("Sing once again with me...") but Jon has terminal vibrato here, and Killian/Lucy have highly synchronised terminal vibrato that appears roughly around the fourth beat but appears earlier with each stanza as each is more intense, and Jon/Lily gradually gets to full vibrato in the last two stanzas (especially trilled in the last one). Holly and James Gant also had it, which means it was already around in 2021. Both OLC and 25RAH didn't have terminal vibrato, both are mostly just full vibrato with variety in trilled frequency.

Interesting.🧐

The Music of the Night and the first unmasking

The Music of the Night: Christine's shuddering inhale, THAT chokehold pose aka he doesn't know how to hug, the gentle smile on her face... and he LITERALLY only has eyes for her and that's how it's gonna be FOREVER!!! AND THE EYEBROW RAISE? JON? SIR🥹He briefly adjusted his mask, which was a pretty compelling detail. He would go up on a few notes in some words, such as when he levels the second half of the "your" in "let your mind start a journey to a strange new world" to the same key as "mind". He was a centimetre away from touching her but then pulled away and seems pretty scared when Christine faces him which is SUCH a good detail. During "floating, falling" he brushed against her belly and then they turned away and I was just screaming inside. He was literally whimpering when Christine touched him while he was on the portcullis (after that, I wasn't sure I would survive Point of No Return🤣). I loved how during portcullis she grabs his hand and slides her hands on his wrists and keeps her distance instead of wholly pressing her body against him (which is an alternative take, both excel in their own ways), as a reinforcement of their initial (spiritual) distance.

Also she runs away after she caresses his cuffs for a good couple of seconds and I felt like it was an interesting take on the canon "his hands feel and smell like death" idea. Their height difference is super cute here, especially when he's leaning on the portcullis, Lily has to stretch up to see him.

She seemed unsure about him touching her at first, especially when they went in for THAT iconic pose, but then she relaxed and just fell against his arm. Her eyes snap open the second he mutters "touch me", so THAT'S where her brain is at, mhm, Christine?😏Her hand lingered on his mask for a good couple of seconds during "savor each sensation", but then it looked like her thumb went underneath the edge and was about to yank it off, and that was when the Phantom pulled her hand away. After Mirror Bride Shock Attack Christine collapsed and he reached out for her, (then looked so adorably confused as she just lay there) before grabbing the cloak and covering her up. Then he knelt down next to her with his hands on his knees like a lil kid to sing "you alone can make my song take flight" it was so adorable and so sad 🥹

During "I Remember" Jon has some aggressive clanking and throws his hands up in satisfaction to reach for his quill, which was a super cute move. When Christine first lays eyes on his deformity, she doesn't flinch until he lunges at her. But then later, after she returned his mask, she flinched right away as if expecting him to lunge at her again🥺She pushed herself as far away from him as she could when he began STYDI but faced him all the time. Jon's voice sounded so scary, but his "dreams of beauty" and "oh, Christine" were soft sobs and whimpers🥹After "*sniff* oh, Christine…" he covered his face with both hands and sat on his knees with his back to the audience, they're so close it looks like they're about to kiss. When Christine handed him the mask, he ripped it out of her hand and put it back on and gave his hair a quick slick back. (GILLIAN LYNNE I FEEL YOU THIS IS TOTALLY- *scream*) and there is NOBODY who does Stranger Than You Dreamt It like this dude I swear the way he sobs and begs as he crawls and grovels and literally drags himself across the stage is IMPECCABLE!!

Absolutely mopping the stage. Not a speck of dust left. Dyson the heck outta that lair, my sewer man (what am I writing)

I love Jon's unique acting choice that I think only he has done during STYDI where he takes a beat and shows his hands like "I mean no harm" before grabbing Christine's wrist for "come, we must return." Totally obsessed with this. It worked really well because Lily's Christine was especially fearful during this scene and I don't blame her because Jon was VERY scary.

During Il Muto Jon's laugh was so deep and dark. Sent chills up my spine. When the scary silhouettes of the Phantom were showing up during the ballet of Il Muto, they were spotted by the shepherd dancer and he looked SO SCARED. He had on this huge nervous smile, you could tell he was thinking "oh God please no" it was just such a golden piece of acting XD

Rooftop and graveyard

During rooftop (especially "All I Ask of You") Christine was very up-front about what she wants from Raoul. During "say you love me every waking moment" it was like saying "these are the terms, you can either agree to them or be done". And having both a stubborn Christine and a stubborn Raoul seems like a difficult thing to make work, but I think it did in this show, especially in Notes II! Their clash during Notes II makes Christine's storming out more realistic as she has lost all her patience and just needs to be alone. Also, I think it was a minor flub but during AIAOY reprise after a particularly intense kiss I think they pulled away too late and Lily had to level "I must go" down by one octave but regained the normal key during "they'll wonder where I am".😏Jon Robyns was literally suffering during AIAOY reprise, hitting his ears and whimpering "no no no" and really emphasizes "denied me and betrayed me".❤️❤️

Wishing was such a VIBE: it's truly Christine's song and it's the only one that doesn't reprise, Lily does it in such a good way and she's literally crying😭one of the most beautiful songs in the history of music. And I suppose that's what makes Phantom THE best: when you listen to each song you think "this one is the best" and by the end you'll have to go "the whole thing is the best".👑

The Point of No Return and Final Lair

The Point of No Return: How. Do. I. Even. Begin. To. Explain. This. Song. I melted in my seat, just as I expected🤣Jon's "preeeeeey" was so long and creepy👻Erik is literally the epitome of "I'm ready for this in theory but utterly unprepared in practice" during the entire song. Nice Italian accent for the Piangi disguise. When Lily starts her part, she has a long pause and a particularly aggressive trill of vibrato in the "you" in "you have brought me". She would stare at the Phantom in confusion, then suddenly face the audience and visibly shake herself, like she was going, "I'm on stage! Stay in-character!" which is a nice detail as it gives a compelling reason why Christine would continue despite her suspicions (if my memory serves me Sierra has also done this and I really like it). I also really love the pacing of this song in this show, the slight tempo shifts adds so much to the already existing tension. She doesn't actually ever try to escape. It's more accurate in that she is aware of the situation and plays along.

You could tell the moment she realized it was the Phantom under the cloak, a look of understanding passed over her face. In the pause before she starts singing her verse, she was thinking for a bit, and then her "decided…" was so long and sounded like a sigh. After she unmasks him he was so shaky as he proposed to her, I was DYING😭it's nice when Christines look shocked at tearing off both the mask and the wig. Makes it more believable that they would just freeze and make it easy for the Phantom to grab them. Then he directly drags her off. So neat. Love this one.

Final Lair: Christine is literally scolding Erik, she's so done🤣And Erik was done too, he had so much disdain in his voice when he talked about his backstory ("this face which earned a mother's fear and loathing, a mask, my first unfeeling scrap of clothing"), his bitterness felt so real and justified. It always hits pretty hard when Erik essentially goes if I'm prettier you would love me, then Christine points out his face isn't exactly the biggest problem here😅When Raoul came in, Erik's mood switched COMPLETELY to being an annoying jerk. He was having way too much fun teasing Raoul and mock-bowing to him and being a brat. His voice was dripping with sarcasm (oh btw they changed "I had rather hoped that you would come" to "I had hardly dreamt we'd be so blessed", I think I prefer the former) and it's brilliant :D "Make. Your. Choice." Chills🥶 I love the way he delivers it as imposing and menacing but also very broken and very scared XD He throws Christine around a lot by grabbing her wrists.

The kiss was so SOB the desperation as he clings to her is like🤯After the kiss there has NEVER been a reaction as intense as this brilliant man Jon Robyns's, he just shoves himself away and clamps his hands over his mouth and Lily touches her lips and they're both like WHAT DID WE JUST DO??! I can't explain how perfect it just is😭After he let Raoul go, he had to chase them out and his "GO NOW!! GO NOW AND LEAVE ME!!!" was the DEFINITION of heartbreaking😭😭His "Christine, I love you" was so long and was even more💔Lily did NOT want to leave him. The little sobs he let out as he watched Raoul and Christine leave on the boat😫and he covered his ears again like in AIAOY reprise. I was convinced she was choosing the Phantom by the end she was practically dragged out of the Lair. I really couldn't control my tearducts and I was just crying my eyes out.

Three principals and after the curtain

Jon "I'm gonna post my holiday dates a year in advance" Robyns could switch from being angry and threatening to heartbroken and distraught instantaneously and it was just so amazing it gives pure Leroux vibes🤩in the middle of a song he has this guttural growl (the "side" in "inside your mind" is the best example, especially prominent in the Big Night of Musicals earlier this year) and in the same sentence he just whimpers (actually sounds like crying) it's AWESOMEE he is literally Leroux!Erik: violent, unpredictable, cries a lot... Gotta say if Hugh Panaro has an eternal smirk with the mask Jon has an eternal scowl with the mask XD Jon has this slight nasal tone which was a bit distracting at times but overall it was alright. His cape fling and✨magical Phantom hands✨were all very *mmm* Kudos for the "far-reaching gaze" instead of "fathering gaze" during "Wandering Child". Excellent noises in Final Lair. He was on fire during the last 20 minutes, this man😭he sobbed into the veil (which is real mood) after Christine left with Raoul and he really put everything into it and he made such good choices, when it was AIAOY reprise he not only put his hands on his ears but also cried out and was saying "no no no" and had this low growling, trust me it was perfect.

Would have been better if he shrieked "Damn you! Curse you!!" in "I Remember" (right before STYDI) rather than to sing those lines, as at that point he's literally breaking apart. Also it would be better to scream "grave" in "So do you end your days with me, or do you send him to his grave" line. He manages to achieve the ghost-like, floaty Phantom who brings the power when it matters, and make very subtle changes with their breath control to convey emotion (especially when pleading with Christine).

Also very pretty deformity (and if we say he has a pretty deformity it means the more gruesome it is the better🤣). In Final Lair I was already breaking apart then the Monkey music box came on and Jon started sobbing himself and I turned into, quite literally, a tap. Final Lair emotional destruction. Gotta love this dude🤌He mastered the balance between a terrifying force of nature and an emotionally (and physically) broken man. His emotions felt real as he said or sang each line. He meant. Every. Word. He was saying.

Lily Kerhoas has pretty much the right amount of vibrato and her anger in Final Lair was very precious. She's excited about meeting the Angel in "The Mirror" and has a wanting and longing in "Enter at LAST master" (in a way that the one and only💗Sarah Brightman💗had done and we haven't had many Christines who do this). Didn't really like her Star Princess (Masquerade) dress; a shorter bodice would be so much better looking and the lack of horizontal V-shaped beading drives me bonkers. The sleeve drape is pretty concerning, it would be better to add some more fluff and structure and fasten them higher up. During Notes II she was trying to convey how Christine is tired of all the stuff happening around her during this scene. Also her "tears of hate" before the trio in Final Lair was sublime :DD She has a huge fluffy wig and its really pretty (it looks natural!), also a very big floofy wedding dress. She starts out very shy during Hannibal and "Little Lotte" and only during MotN do we start to see her come out of her shell.

She learns to become more confident and to stand up for herself. I really like this, because too often Christine is written off as a sweet innocent ingenue seduced by Erik (which is partially true but some take it too far😅), but Lily had her own goals in mind and held onto them stubbornly, which led to her having the upper hand in a lot of scenes. She is also truly gorgeous and has a beautifully smooth and sweet voice. Very strong Christine (even stronger than Emilie Kouatchou), near calling a lot of the shots. On the travelator during the title track she actually goes ahead of Phantom by several feet like "so we're going to your place, right?". During PoNR after she feels his mask and it's he who pulls away from her she's the one putting his hands on her waist and pressing herself against him like "ahem, you started this fire and now I'm going to pour some gasoline on it." Absolutely love her.

Joe Griffiths-Brown made a dashing Vicomte, a bit wooden at times, particularly in Notes I and Think of Me, but generally I really liked his performance (especially during AIAOY and Masquerade). REALLY loved him in AIAOY, he was so charming towards Christine and it was very sweet, it made a Team Raoul for the extent of the song🥂During the auction, when the music box came out, he leaned forward and touched it longingly with his fingertips🥺And he spoke in this hushed, gravelly old man voice that was so accurate. Notes I was where he began to show his colors as a more aggressive Raoul. While the managers are busy singing "Prima Donna" with Carlotta and Piangi, Raoul was chatting with Madame Giry and makes their little one-on-one after Masquerade seem less random because we know they are already familiar with each other. His voice is strong and cuts through all the rest of the voices and could be clearly heard at the end of "Prima Donna". During AIAOY he did a twirl with his arms out as he sang "then say you’ll share with me" and it was the sweetest thing ever🥹AND THE KISS!!!

Raoul kissed Christine first, but after he pulled away Christine leaned in and gave him a bigger kiss back!! FOR A MOMENT I JUSTIFIED RAOULSTINE🤣💗💗He's a serious and determined Raoul that reminds me a lot of Hadley Fraser, like an arrogant puppy. He has his moments of sweetness and is very protective of Christine during AIAOY and Final Lair.

Overall, this is A MOMENT OF MY LIFE and so brilliant overall I cried for WHO KNOWS HOW LONG. It was SO GOOD. I cannot describe how perfect it was in so many different ways. No wonder Jon/Lily/Joe are the principal E/C/R's; their understanding and expression of the music is sublime. The entire cast and crew were nothing short of PHENOMENAL.

OH WAIT forgot to mention: during "Wandering Child" the Phantom's staff shoots little sparks but then at the end a huge row of fire appears from the floor and everyone just screamed. It was such a genuinely scary moment.
