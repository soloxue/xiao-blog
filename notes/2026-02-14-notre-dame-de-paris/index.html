Notre-Dame de Paris: Frollo, again

“This run was perfect. It felt so complete.”

Translated from the Chinese post

« Laissez mon âme s’envoler
Loin des misères de la terre
Laisser mon amour se mêler
À la lumière de l’univers »
—— 🔔 𝙉𝙤𝙩𝙧𝙚-𝘿𝙖𝙢𝙚 𝙙𝙚 𝙋𝙖𝙧𝙞𝙨 🔔 ——

Today was a god-tier performance in every possible way. I’ll keep my blabbing to three posts at most — mostly wildly praising the great Lavoie 🙊 I kept my binoculars trained on him. With such familiar people and such a familiar show, there’s really not much to say: I can recite the entire musical by now. NDDP is the greatest musical in human history.

My fifth NDP viewing! (I don’t think the one in fourth grade counts 😂) I’ve seen three performances this run. I’m so happy, and I genuinely don’t know how I’ll recover from this withdrawal 😭 Everyone was adding vocal embellishments today. Absolutely god-tier! Flo has improved so much since 2024: his voice is bright and resonant. His “Le Temps des cathédrales” sounded, appropriately, like Notre-Dame after its restoration — what am I saying? “La Fête des fous” and “Le Val d’amour” were so joyous! “Lune” was a little too upright and forceful, but I’m biased about that song, so I’ll leave it there, haha. Angelo has lost so much weight — surely I’m not imagining it? His face wasn’t this angular when I saw him in 2024. He even seems thinner than at last year’s NDP 25th-anniversary reunion 🙀 He’s still the rock-solid anchor of the show. “Danse mon Esmeralda” made me cry so much 🥹 Oh, speaking of Phoebus: Damien, I was wrong. I miss Captain Damien T_T. I’ll never call you bald again — although that seventy-something youngster really does have more hair than you!!

Martin’s voice is so powerful that the first line of “Déchiré” startled me every time. Later he may have realised and started pinching his voice; I couldn’t hear the high notes in “Belle” at all. Damien, may I apologise? I love you. I know I was wrong 🙇 Jay was as reliable as ever. “Les Sans-Papiers”, “La Cour des miracles” and “Condamnés” — with embellishments! — were so satisfying! Elhaida’s dancing in “Bohémienne” was as beautiful as always, and the lighting for “Vivre” remained utterly divine. (During “Ave Maria païen”, I watched poor Frollo curled up in his little square the entire time, hahaha. Daniel’s expression and that outstretched hand — heartbreak 💔💔.) Emma’s “Ces diamants-là” — flourishes! — and “La Monture” — flourishes! — were so much better than last year. Her expression while that cheating man made excuses did NOT look like acting. I need to go home and listen to lots of Patrick to cleanse my ears, aaaaaah 😦 Everyone was embellishing in “Fatalité”. Absolutely, absolutely perfect!!!

Well↗️ well, well, Lord Claude Frollo. Who isn’t biased in Daniel’s favour? But how is this man, who’ll turn 77 in a little over a month, THIS good? It’s frightening! He’s completely in control of the Archdeacon’s songs now. He can play with the rhythm however he likes and still stay on beat, especially in “Belle” and “Un matin tu dansais”. The Archdeacon’s arrogance was at full power. I especially love watching him fling that robe around. You can tell he really likes this costume; after all, he once complained that the original robe made him look like Batman, hahaha. And now that his hair is white, he looks like a saint. How could anyone hate him 😭 He still marks the beat with the accompaniment, and not only during his rock entrance. In “La Sorcière” and all those commands, the lyrics still tumble out like they’re burning his tongue, hhh. Daniel really is the pape du rap — what a cursed joke 🙊 The lowered key, changed lyrics and slower pace of “Ananké” still take me out of it 🙀 “Belle”, aaaaaah, was perfect.

All that conflict, agony, devotion and heartbreak. What am I going to do if you stop performing NDP one day 😭 The “chihuahua” number had especially fierce embellishments today; now I understand how he lost his voice in the first week 😂😭 Daniel: *pant* *pant* *vocal flourish* *pant* *vocal flourish*. Hahaha, unbelievable. “Florence” is still divine. It’s Daniel’s favourite song, and I barely looked at Gringoire during it — forgetting my roots a bit there. Today’s “Être prêtre et aimer une femme” was especially heartbreaking. How many recordings has this man swallowed? How can he sound the same as he did 27 years ago?? That song seems consistently to catch him at his best. As the lights dimmed at the end, you could see his silhouette walking offstage. Without the outer robe in that number, his outline looked so young: still so tall and long-legged — he was over 1.9 metres tall when he was younger. Aaaaaah, I wanted to cry 😭 Then his walk suddenly became so swaggering as he headed offstage that I immediately saw ✨certain✨ early music videos — ahem, “La Danse du Smatte”.

Old Daniel’s rock ’n’ roll heart lives on 🙊 Oh, and today’s “je t’AIIIIIIME”: how did he hold it longer AND more steadily than 27 years ago? Are you really 76?? “Un matin tu dansais” is another favourite! During their confrontation in the cage, every time Esmeralda yelled “va-t’en”, she shoved him hard. Bishop Daniel caught her hand every time and followed her with his eyes. Daniel Lavoie, tu vas me détruire — actually, tu m’as déjà détruit… When Clopin died in “L’Attaque de Notre-Dame”, he walked over, made the sign of the cross, then quietly walked away. He didn’t cover his ears like Solal. In “Mon Maître, mon Sauveur”, Frollo was already close to a breakdown when he admitted that he’d killed Esmeralda. But the most astonishing moment came when Quasimodo pushed him from the bell tower: first a scream of an intensity I genuinely couldn’t believe he could produce, then that crazed laugh finally turning into a sob. Gérald, just say it if you want me to die in this theatre — wait, why am I calling him Gérald?

My apologies to Mr Yvan Dufresne, ahem—

It was perfect. This run felt so complete 😭 Daniel Lavoie, are you some kind of immortal? Your career has lasted more than three times my age, yet every time I watch you perform, I feel like I’m the 76-year-old 🙀 I hope I can see you again, whether in NDP, in concert or at a Quebec music festival. What am I going to do without you…
