Notre-Dame de Paris: the reunion

“Thank you for letting Notre-Dame de Paris travel across time, space and languages.”

Screen viewing · Translated from the Chinese post, including its update

NOTRE DAME DE PARIS — Les Grandes Retrouvailles — 25 Ans Après

(Although I don’t understand why 2025 − 1998 = 25 — maybe they left out the COVID years, or counted from the start of the tour?? But… 👇)

YAYYYYY, our NDDP finally has a professionally filmed 25th-anniversary reunion!!! And it’s the ORIGINAL CAST!!! Almost all my favourite French-musical performers are here! The combined ages of my six original-cast favourites are nearly 350 🤯 And they’re still amazing! (And they sang three more dinners!! 👉👈)

Photo 1: Daniel’s own vertical post was too blurry, so I had to borrow the landscape version from M6. The funniest part is that I actually watched TV5’s version, which had been available since the 18th, because I simply couldn’t wait until M6’s scheduled broadcast on the 29th. I battled a painfully slow Toronto VPN connection and those annoying adverts, and in the end I WON 😏

Everyone’s speeches after “Danse mon Esmeralda” were so, so moving 🥹 “Thank you for letting Notre-Dame de Paris travel across time, space and languages.” Then everyone started saying merci: to Richard and Luc, to the producers, to the band, to all the audience… It was so beautiful 😭😭

Et vraiment… “D’un seul regard, tu as mis le feu à ma vie” ❤️‍🔥

(Yes, yes, let’s try to ignore the fact that this line comes from “La Volupté” ಡωಡ)

Still, it felt strange without Luck… Why is his case still not over 😅 Leaving his private conduct aside, he really is a wonderful artist and the best Clopin. We’ll have another chance to hear his “Les Sans-Papiers” and “Condamnés”, right?! This is the one downside of being addicted to the original cast, aaaaaah.

“Le Blues du businessman” sounded so good! I forgot to take a photo 😭

Patrick kept adding vocal flourishes! Apparently he was having the time of his life showing off in “Déchiré” 😆 He couldn’t even stop his leg bouncing. The little sun has become a GIANT sun 🤣 Patrick, you’ve changed the most of this original-cast group. Where is that Phoebus in The Making of Notre-Dame de Paris who yelled “Déchiré” in the recording booth and then startled himself into repeated “oh là là”s 🥹 Why are you so broad now, my God? All those “exasperated dad” shuttle runs haven’t helped (Damien! Teach him, quick).

It’s so rare to see “Tu vas me détruire” without the pushing-open-the-lift-doors move — strike that! Daniel, I absolutely have to see you again next year!!

Oh, yesterday was Gian’s 39th birthday! Happy belated birthdayyy. I really, really hope I can see you again next year! *˘︶˘* 🎂🎂🎂🎂

Right, time for me to complain… These producers are so infuriating. They cut “Beau comme le soleil”, “Ces diamants-là”, “Les Oiseaux qu’on met en cage” AND “La Monture”. What are you doing?! Did Julie offend you somehow? You’ve deleted practically all her songs! And “La Manic” too — what did Bruno ever do to you 🤬 Most importantly, what do you MEAN by cutting “Lune”?? Isn’t that Bruno’s favourite song?! (As someone whose WeChat ID is literally a line from “Lune”… it’s my favourite too… 😭) If Bruno hadn’t fought so hard to save it when songs were being cut from the show back then, NDDP wouldn’t even have this song! And it matched his state of mind at the time, so nobody can replace his version!! Whoever did this, do you have a heart 😡😡😡😡 My “Lune”… I’ll say tabarnak on Bruno’s behalf 🤬🤬 I cannot believe this. Stop trying to cut the Québécois / Canadian legacy out of Notre-Dame de Paris 🤣 Last year’s French broadcast of Starmania treated him badly too… And where is the final encore chorus of “Le Temps des cathédrales”??

Straight to “Danse mon Esmeralda” — doesn’t your conscience hurt!! Now I can only pray there’ll be a Quebec version like last year’s Starmania. Please, I’m begging you, don’t copy the French version’s song-cutting spree and shitty editing 💔

Basically, this is the meme for it 🤬 [animated sticker]

Give me back “Lune”.

Update: M6 has rescued the dignity TV5 threw away, hahaha. It included “Lune”, “Beau comme le soleil”, “Ces diamants-là” and “La Monture”, making it over twenty minutes longer than TV5’s version! Bruno’s “Lune” truly is irreplaceable 😭😭 But still no “La Manic”. Singing “La Manic” in Quebec really means something; I don’t understand why it was cut… [image]

A miracle in French-musical history: how did Richard and Luc look at a smooth-talking penniless poet, an already balding creep and literally the ugliest man in Paris back in ’97 and go, yup, we need three of the hottest people in the francophone world on this RIGHT NOW.
