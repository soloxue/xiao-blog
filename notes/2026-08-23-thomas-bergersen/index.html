Concert · Shanghai · 23 Aug 2026

Victory, live.

“Goosebumps from the very first note, and they never went away.”

After the concert · Translated from the Chinese

Haven’t been to a concert in ages — last one was probably JOJ in ’24 😮 Hearing Thomas Bergersen in a concert hall was so good. And who says a hall’s visuals can’t match an arena’s? The lighting was absolutely unbeatable (condolences to the front-row audience getting swept back and forth by the beams), and the hall’s sound field and acoustics were far stronger — so immersive!

The fusion of classical orchestra and electronic music was perfect; I fully understood what it looks like when a group of genius musicians play around with music. At one point Thomas finished at the piano, immediately grabbed a pick and switched to electric guitar — he’s got this one-man band thing down (and the violin, electric piano and maracas behind him hahaha). Mia’s bow-spinning was so cool (+ at one point, their lightsaber bows 🐶). Uyanga and Merethe’s harmonies were divine 🙀 — and the two of them were so energetic, dancing the whole time hahaha.

Empire of Angels: goosebumps from the very first note, and they never went away — I think I was turning into a chicken 🐶 During Strength of a Thousand Men I spent the whole time staring at the Carnyx horn Saulius was holding hahaha, such a distinctively shaped Celtic war horn.

Star Sky & Victory always bring back so many memories 🥹 — good thing I haven’t been brainwashed by the marketing accounts; a lot of them don’t deserve BGMs like these 😇 Away with your Fairies was very Scottish — midway through, Mia, Thomas and Tina had a play-off, even drawing their bows on each other’s instruments; I was stunned lmaoo. Flight of the Silverbird was too beautiful 😭 I love Saulius’s two million different flutes hahaha.

El Dorado reminded me of last year’s math drama again — that was my curtain-call song! So many memories 🥹 In Memoria, Merethe’s voice was so deeply moving.

Impossible was, I think, the GOAT of tonight! The heavy metal in the second half was so, so cool — I fully felt the magic of live music.

Heart of Courage: full marks for live impact; everyone was having a blast, you could tell. Pixies was also great, a really good encore. And the audience’s energy today was especially good. Also, since the hall doesn’t allow filming, everyone was immersed — not all holding up their phones. A truly enjoyable evening!
