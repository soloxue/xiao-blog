Chinese musical · Note dated 30 May 2026

The Orphan of Zhao

“This is what an encore is for.”

Translated from the Chinese post

“At times, heaven and earth split open without warning;
fate is mercilessly drawn inside. Lightning strikes the bell;
the drowsy still lose themselves in beautiful dreams.”
—⚔— The Orphan of Zhao —⚔—

A show with really good big musical numbers: “Song of Fate”, “Absolutely Not”, “The Flying Dragon Sleeps upon the Roof Tiles”, “The World” and “Guarding the Frontier” — that reprise could go straight onto the Spring Festival Gala — all sounded so good! Every character’s showcase song made me cry… Cheng Ying, his wife, Zhao Dun, Han Jue, Gongsun Chujiu, Wei Jiang: you’re all heroes 😭 Through my binoculars I could often see that the actors were really crying. Special praise for Zheng Qiyuan: I finally got to see him live! Even singing bent over didn’t affect him at all. Why did I want to cry whenever he spoke 🥹 His frantic scurrying felt so real, especially when he was terrified by all the bodies covering the stage. “You Love Me” was so painful. Could you please NOT smile with that look of release? I was in the THIRD ROW, aaaaaah. His tiny expressions could kill me even without binoculars. “You don’t deserve to find my grave.” “Yes. I don’t deserve anything.” 😭

I love this show’s ensemble and stage. There are really only walls, picture frames and rocks, but combined with the lighting, they’re wonderful. In “Song of Fate”, when the lyric goes “flesh and blood turn to white bones”, everyone inside the frames turns and walks forward together. Goosebumps everywhere 😮 I suppose I just love characters like Cheng Ying’s son’s soul — ahem, La Mort in the French Roméo et Juliette. This entire show is so Shakespearean. Resisting the urge to tack on a perfectly happy ending isn’t easy. I’m so happy to see a Chinese musical like this (*˘︶˘*). But those lyrics… the quality is wildly uneven. The person who wrote “How vast the realm; heroes are as tiny as grains of sand” also wrote WHAT? “I am Tu’an Gu; who dares block my path?” Bro, are you trying to be Don Quixote? “Hurry up and take a few more breaths while you can” — ??? And Cheng Bo’s “Your Highness, my birth mother!” makes me wonder what dodgy liquor the lyricist was drinking. In the second half, “He’s my orphan”, “You’re my son”, “I’m your son”, “He’s my son” had me completely dizzy.

Whose son AM I? Still, the good writing really was powerful. Someone who doesn’t understand Chinese would probably have an amazing time listening 😂 And I realised the advantage of a sung-through musical: so much of the spoken dialogue was hard to hear, and they didn’t provide subtitles. Testing the audience’s Classical Chinese reading comprehension alone isn’t enough, aaaaaah.

I can’t imagine how miserable I’d have been without “The Radiance of Life” at the end… Practically every character onstage has died by then 😭 The live chorus was more powerful than the studio recording. This is what an encore is for: bring everyone straight back to life and let them sing such a hopeful song together 🥹 So good, so good, so good. (I nearly typed “The Light of Life”. Faust, look what you’ve done to me 😂.)

(BTW, why is this show three and a half hours long? I don’t think I’ve ever seen a musical this long before.)

Such an adorable medicine chest. Cheng Ying 😭😭😭 The greatest physician — wait, why does saying it like that sound so strange 🐶 [image]

(One more compliment for allowing filming during the encore but not during the bows, like Faust. Hearing the whole theatre filled with applause really does feel different 🥹.)\
