Concert · Shanghai · 24 Sep 2026

A night of French musicals.

“The moment I heard the overture, I was ready to return to 1482.”

After the concert · Translated from the Chinese

💙🤍❤️ “I’d like to see the world upside down, if perhaps it were more beautiful.”

In short, French musicals really, really are perfect for dancing. I had the best time listening to a rock-musical concert tonight 😈 When Hélène came out for Monopolis, it felt like a strange collision of time periods. When are we getting all seven original Notre-Dame de Paris cast members back together? (Though only six can still return now… 😭)

Robert, are you inheriting Daniel’s businessman-blues-to-Frollo trajectory? Hahaha. SOS d’un terrien en détresse was exquisite. I support a Pierre Gringoire–Johnny Rockfort double bill (cough, cough, Bruno). There was so much Starmania on the programme and I was delighted. But speaking of which, Macron, where is my Starmania China tour? 😡

Angelo was genuinely powerful in Le Rouge et le Noir; giving him only Quasimodo to sing was a waste. I support Elhaida bringing Salaud into Notre-Dame de Paris and singing it at Phoebus. (Gian came out halfway through and just served face for two straight minutes, LMAO.) The Romeo and Juliet songs were even more rock. In Vérone and Les Rois du monde, Gian was visibly having a wonderful time. David, watch out: this Italian named Angelo may steal your job 🙊

In Aimer, Hélène sounded so much like Joy Esther that I was shocked. The whole song made me picture the dancers and chorus from the Romeo and Juliet sequence and took me back to December 😭 (And when is the revival?)

The Notre-Dame de Paris part of the second half was such a pleasure. Before it began they played an Ouverture and MY HEART NEARLY EXPLODED. I miss NDP so much. The moment I heard the overture, I was ready to return to 1482. Le Temps des cathédrales — I miss Gian’s poet so much. I haven’t seen him live since January 2025. When is he returning to NDP in France? 😇

Hélène’s Bohémienne took me straight back to 1998. She was only 27 then, and even after 28 years Esmeralda is still in her 😭 She looked so warmly at Elhaida when they sang together. Belle and L’Oiseau qu’on met en cage were beautiful too. Imagine hearing Hélène and Garou for the first time in 1998.

Robert’s “Chihuahua” had something of Daniel about it, though was he having trouble with the French r? It really became “Chihuahua de TUI” AHAHAHA. During the Molière section, I completely understood why Abi has so many fans. Don Juan’s flamenco sound was wonderful. And believe it or not, this was almost my first time hearing the whole of “Kill Me, I Beg You.” Angelo has set a very high bar 😈

It was such a wonderful evening with these brilliant francophones 😭 (It’s funny how few of the people onstage were actually French, hahaha.) Oh, right — Gian led Le Temps des cathédrales at the end! It was so emotional 🥹 Apart from Abi, the six performers today were practically having a Notre-Dame de Paris reunion.

And I was dressed like a French flag today, in blue, white and red. Quelle coïncidence 🐶
