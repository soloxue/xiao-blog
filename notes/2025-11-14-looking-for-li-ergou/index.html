Looking for Li Ergou

“Using light to stand in for bullets was genius.”

Translated from the Chinese post

“A breeze sweeps down from the ridge; wildflowers bloom beneath the firs.”
— Looking for Li Ergou
Rachel and I were discussing the timelines of this show and The Message HAHAHA
#Meixihu gang 2/4 😈😈😈

Qiyu (although honestly, calling him anything other than Zhang Chao still feels so strange 👉👈) really suits this character! I borrowed Rachel’s binoculars and caught so many adorable little details! “Long Slumber” was incredible 🔥🔥 Zhang Huifang is amazing: I could hear her clearly through that huge ensemble. Her very first note made me think of Juliet again, hahaha 🙊 Her harmonies with Chen Bohao were so beautiful!!!

The stage design is SO GOOD!! Everyone was adorable while introducing the different guns in “I Have a Gun”, omg. And then “You’re the one who fired at an American military plane?!” was hilarious 🤣 “Seumnida” was the MVP HAHAHA, that song really stuck with me 😆 I loved the rock vibes of “The Contest”, and using light to stand in for bullets was genius 😻 The lighting in that number was incredible, so electrifying! When the red flag appeared, it felt as though everything lit up! “Letter Home” made me cry so much… I think I’ve seen too many adaptations of Korean musicals; THIS felt like a truly Chinese musical!! *high-pitched shriek* “The Poem I Once Couldn’t Understand” and its later reprise sounded so good. Why did they remind me a little of “Absolutely Not” from The Orphan of Zhao, lmao? “The Sniper Battle” was the all-round MVP: music, lighting, stage design, EVERYTHING!!! It felt like everyone onstage was having the time of their lives 🔥 And singing “My Motherland” together afterwards was absolutely overwhelming 🙀🙀 Just those few of you had more presence than an entire Spring Festival Gala, my God.

I really admire anyone who can sing that song together without it feeling the least bit like slogan-chanting. Put these kids on the Gala.

Oh, right, Chang Weiguo was hilarious. A “tri-fold phone”, “Ice and Snow World”, “a lifetime hot-spring membership” — WHAT even were those HAHAHA, I loved it so much.

My little Chang Weiguo fox is so cute [animated sticker]
