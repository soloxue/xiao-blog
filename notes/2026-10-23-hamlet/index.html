Play · Shanghai · 23 Oct 2026

Hamlet.

“The ending of this production was so distinctive. I loved it.”

After the performance · Translated from the Chinese

“Whether ’tis nobler in the mind to suffer the slings and arrows of outrageous fortune, or to take arms against a sea of troubles, and by opposing end them?” — Hamlet

It’s impressive that Beijing People’s Art Theatre made this production of Hamlet work so well. It was much more interesting than I expected.

The flashbacks were fascinating. Although it’s a play, it kept feeling like a musical to me: a proper ensemble, choreography, and at one point I thought Claudius was about to start singing 😂 Zheng Yunlong’s lines were so clear that it was as if subtitles were appearing directly in my head. Every change of tone was so precise, too — including the silly high voice when Hamlet pretended to be mad, hahaha.

Ophelia’s costume in the second half was so artistic: torn-up letters and scraps of cloth printed with text. And her acting was so good 😭 When Polonius was lecturing Laertes, she stood beside them, pointed to herself, then started imitating Polonius’s gestures. So cute.

Those ghostly mannequins dropping down gave me a jump scare on the level of seeing Buquet’s body fall in The Phantom of the Opera for the first time 😮

“His face was as pale as his shirt” — and Hamlet was wearing a black shirt. What kind of writing slip is that, bruh?

The “To be or not to be” monologue didn’t feel quite so intensely negative to me. It was more like he was trying to argue with himself.

Then, in the second half, came the famous “Ah, I’m dead.” Polonius was in that cabinet for a whole two minutes while Hamlet and Gertrude were being philosophical. Then he suddenly fell out and said, “Ah, I’m dead!” (There was even another one later.) It was hilarious 😂

This production’s ending was so distinctive. After the duel it launched straight into stream of consciousness: everyone began delivering the “To be or not to be” monologue, their lines overlapping, until Hamlet concluded with “To be or not to be.” I loved it 😭

When Hamlet went mad, I kept seeing Murata Taiki in my head. Aah, now I miss The Magic Hour again. There was even a little cart 😆 His scream while threatening Polonius, and his conversation with the players about adding a bit to their play, both reminded me so much of Murata Taiki. I half expected the next line to be, “Where’s my eyeliner?” 😇

His flute playing was adorable, and his interactions with Rosencrantz and Guildenstern were amazing. “Pri—mate!” *strikes pose* And is The Magic Hour coming back next year? 😇
