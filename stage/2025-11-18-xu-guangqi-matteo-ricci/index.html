Original play · Director · 18 Nov 2025

Xu Guangqi & Matteo Ricci

“I’m proud of you, and proud of the power of theatre.”

After the performance · Translated from the Chinese

The Mathematics Drama Club’s original 2025 play, Xu Guangqi and Matteo Ricci, has had its complete performance. That’s a wrap! 🔚[celebration][celebration]

We really were amazing. How did I end up with such a wonderful team? 🥹🫶🫶

I don't think I can ever stress this enough but every moment spent with you guys has been joy encapsulated and I love you guys so so so much💋💋💋💋

From writing the script over the summer to every rehearsal after school started and finally putting all the pieces together, we all gave so much time. Everyone squeezed in work on the play around a heavy school workload. I kept chasing you down to quiz you on your lines; the tech team had to revise the background animation again and again; and even Lord Jin, despite a broken bone, couldn’t retire from the cast because I promptly wrote pages tailored to him… Thank you all for working so hard!

I know these months brought us happiness too. We created together, rehearsed together, worried together, and grew together. I’m proud of you, and proud of the power of theatre.

Thank you again to our wonderful actors, costume, makeup and props team, tech team, volunteers, and the school leaders for their strong support. Xu Guangqi and Matteo Ricci could not have succeeded without you! [hearts][hearts]

As director, I’ve been working on this project since July: revising the script, casting, coordinating with every department, making sound effects, checking that lines were memorised, rehearsing, handling logistics, creating scene illustrations, writing separate scripts for sound, props and lighting, and supervising everything. I did all of that. It was exhausting, and it made me so happy.

To the 27 students onstage and behind the scenes, and to all the teachers who helped: every one of you matters. You were all. So. Good. Today, so I won’t mention anyone by name 💋 I selfishly posted lots of photos of myself, but you all looked so beautiful and handsome today—go download your photos, hahaha. Special thanks to Teacher Aliang and Teacher Kristine for the photos!! You’re incredible 😻😻

Thank you for all your work today!! Extra drumsticks for everyone 🍗🍗 I hope we can make theatre together again! cast, crew, creatives — you guys are the best. teammates. EVER.

I love you all.

— “To surpass, we must first bring things together!” —

I’m really going to miss you all 😭

The black box was completely full today. Thank you to everyone who came to see our show!! I’m so grateful for your love and support [animated sticker]

There were still a few problems in our final run-through this afternoon, but in the performance the actors and the technical setup all surpassed themselves. I sat beside the sound and lighting operators because I had to cue every scene and lighting change. It wasn’t the best seat, but I was so proud of you all [hearts][hearts][hearts]
