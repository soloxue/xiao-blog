About

Xiaoxiao

Xiaoxiao is a student who writes fiction and essays in English. Her story The Eighth Rank, her Commedia fantasia Iatrogenesis, and her essay on neuroscience and religious experience — shortlisted by the John Locke Institute — all began as questions she couldn’t put down: about masks, about time, about what it costs to see clearly.

She is a regular in the auditorium — watching far more opera and musical theatre than is strictly necessary — and a regular on stage of another kind, playing in her school band. She writes the way she watches: closely, and from the middle of the crowd.

This site is the notebook itself: fiction, essays and occasional writing, with images drawn for each piece. The lights are up — come in.

This site is set in Bodoni Moda, the face of the nineteenth-century opera poster, and Newsreader, for the long quiet read. It is a notebook, not a monument: things appear when they are ready.
