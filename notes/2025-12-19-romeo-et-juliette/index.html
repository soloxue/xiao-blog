Back in a theatre after a month! I’ve spent nearly a whole year seeing Chinese musicals; my last French one was NDDP in January! This year really began with a French musical and ended with one 😼 The French Roméo et Juliette is genuinely one of my favourite musicals. The music, stage design, scenery — everything is incredible!!!

Today’s outfit was inspired by Romeo!! Although the colours are closer to the Chinese production, hahaha. Why no dress? One word: COLD.

Look closely: this Damien Sargue has returned to his comfort zone!! This man can play both the most faithless Phoebus and the most devoted Romeo 🙊 And Cécilia is even more ridiculous: you haven’t changed AT ALL, you’re still so beautiful 🥰 I think this is one of the few French musicals where I’m not obsessed with having the original cast, apart from Damien and CC. Maybe Cyril and John Eyzen are just THAT good!! Cyril, how do you still look the same as you did over twenty years ago! I miss John Eyzen’s curls. The dreadlocks are very in character, but his old long hair was so beautiful: Verona’s own little madman 😭 All that swishing his skirt — robe — and wriggling around was adorable, and provoking la Nourrice was so funny 🙊 Juju was wonderful today. I love la Nourrice so much: she’s basically Juliette’s cooler mom 😼 And Death, one of my absolute favourite characters, has inspired quite a bit of my own writing, hahaha. I miss her old glittery makeup and hair. She’s a deity, not a ghost 😢

Hearing that low voice in the Ouverture again after all these years — “toutes les histoires, commencent pareil, rien de nouveau sous la lune” — aaaaaah, goosebumps everywhere… “L’amour, y a que ça qui compte / On s’aimera si fort” really is this musical encapsulated 🥹 Death flicked her sleeves and all the stars lit up. Absolutely stunning! “Vérone” is one of my favourite musical songs of all time! David Bán’s Prince had such a commanding presence; I was immersed immediately, as if I’d really travelled to Verona! I especially love “Ici le venin de la haine / coule dans nos vies comme dans nos veines”. The stage design in that song was stunning too! The two mothers’ duet in “La Haine” made such an impression on me years ago. Red Mum and Blue Mum sounded wonderful together.

“Un Jour” was so beautiful, sob sob sob. Damien and Cécilia are Romeo and Juliet forever!! “Les Rois du monde” is such a classic, the ultimate leg-bouncing tune, hahaha. I still remember long-haired Damien and his little alternative-fashion gang — kidding — in leather jackets, through that truly touching video quality. “Kings of the World” forever, aaah 🥹 Every time I remember that of Romeo, Mercutio and Benvolio, only Benvolio will be left, I get so sad 💔 He may be the most miserable person in all Verona, and Cyril’s whole presence makes it even sadder T_T. “La Reine Mab (Je rêve)” afterwards is another favourite. John Eyzen is so powerful!! The one and only Mercutio 💜 The live lighting was incredible, and that wild laugh every time! “J’ai peur” is so easy to relate to. Damien, are you a human recording machine? He used to bend backwards over Death’s arm; now he just leans back a bit. Can’t bend that far any more, hahaha?

The ball is perfect for dancing!! I really love Mercutio and Benvolio’s interactions; they’re adorable!! Grégory’s “C’est pas ma faute” sounded so good. This musical really makes Tybalt understandable 😭 But it was hilarious: he knocked the couples over one pair at a time, while Damien and CC kissed for over three minutes and simply REFUSED to fall over. So funny, aaaaaah 🙊 “Le Balcon” feels very Disney to me, especially that slide before they sing together. One of those songs I’ll always hold dear! “On ne peut pas changer l’histoire / la nôtre commencera ce soir” — what god-tier lyrics 🥰 “Et voilà qu’elle aime” was beautiful. Juju is so good, and the Nurse is such a wonderful person! “Aimer” is simply the greatest love song in musical-theatre history. No objections accepted. “Aimer, et sentir son cœur / Aimer, pour avoir moins peur” — such, such beautiful words 🥹 CC’s purple dress was gorgeous!

I didn’t record any videos of today’s encore, just photos! Honestly, singing and bouncing along with them is more fun than taking pictures! There isn’t another song in the world as rebellious AND romantic as “Avoir 20 ans”! Of everywhere I’ve ever been, this show’s encore may be where I’ve most wanted to get up and dance.

I have to say, Culture Square’s sound system is rubbish, aaaaaah. Even the actors shouting couldn’t compete with it.

I was so happy today. So many people complimented me, hahaha. See you on the 23rd!

Oh, I forgot: “J’ai peur” still featured fifty-metre shuttle runs today. It kept reminding me of that “exasperated dad” routine. Does anyone understand what I mean, aaaaaah?
